# Team management

## Roles
There are four roles: Owner, Admin, Editor and Viewer. Only an Owner can transfer ownership or delete the workspace.

## Seats and invitations
The Free plan includes 5 seats and Pro includes 25 seats. Invitations expire after 7 days if they are not accepted.

## Offboarding
Removing a member immediately revokes the API keys they created. Enterprise customers can automate provisioning with SCIM.
