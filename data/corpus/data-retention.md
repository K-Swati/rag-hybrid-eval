# Data retention

## Metrics
Raw metrics are kept for 30 days on Free, 13 months on Pro and 3 years on Enterprise. Logs are kept for 14 days on every plan.

## Exports and deletion
You can export data as CSV or Parquet at any time. When an account is closed all of its data is purged within 30 days. Encrypted backups are retained for 35 days.

## Legal holds
Enterprise customers can request a legal hold, which pauses deletion for the affected data until the hold is lifted.
