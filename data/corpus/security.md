# Security

## Compliance
Nimbus is SOC 2 Type II certified and a penetration test is performed annually. Vulnerabilities can be reported to security@nimbus.example.

## Encryption and access
Data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and with TLS 1.2 or higher in transit. Two-factor authentication using TOTP apps is available to all users. Enterprise workspaces can enforce SSO through SAML 2.0 and restrict access with an IP allowlist.

## Audit logs
Audit logs record every administrative action and are retained for 12 months.
