# Billing FAQ

## Invoices
Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month. Annual plans can be paid by bank transfer as well as by card. Add your tax ID in the billing settings so that it appears on invoices.

## Overage and refunds
Usage beyond your plan is billed at $0.40 per 1,000 extra events. Annual purchases can be refunded within 14 days of payment.

## Plan changes and failed payments
Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle. If a payment fails we retry 3 times over 7 days and then suspend the workspace.
