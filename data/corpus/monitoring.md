# Monitoring and alerts

## Evaluation
Alert rules are evaluated every 1 minute. Pro workspaces can create up to 200 alert rules.

## Condition types
You can alert on a fixed threshold, on a composite of several conditions, or on anomaly detection. Anomaly detection needs at least 7 days of history before it starts firing.

## Notifications
Alerts can be sent to email, Slack and PagerDuty. Mute windows suppress notifications during planned maintenance without disabling the rule.
